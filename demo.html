How It Works

Device 1 Step 1

Click "Generate QR Code" to create a WebRTC offer. A QR code will appear on the screen containing the connection information.

Device 2 Step 2

Click "Scan QR Code" and point the camera at Device 1's screen. The app will read the offer and automatically create an answer.

Device 2 Step 3

A new QR code appears on Device 2 containing the answer. Show this to Device 1.

Device 1 Step 4

Click "Scan QR Code" and scan Device 2's QR code. The connection will be established!

🎉 Connected!

Both devices are now connected. Use the colored buttons to change the remote participant's background color. Have fun!

💡 Technical Note

This application uses WebRTC for peer-to-peer communication. The QR codes contain the SDP (Session Description Protocol) data needed to establish the connection. Once connected, all data flows directly between devices with no intermediary server. A data channel is used to send control messages for the background color changes.
